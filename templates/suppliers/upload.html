Before you upload

  • Use the Suppliers sheet with the template's 41 columns, from Sl No to Remarks.
  • Vendor Code and Vendor / Firm Name are required; existing vendor codes are rejected.
  • Trade, Material Category, Key Materials Supplied, Brands Dealt and Project(s) Worked On must match existing master data. Separate multiple names with commas or semicolons.
  • Status: Active, On hold or Inactive. Checks: OK/Not OK or Yes/No. Ratings: 0–5. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD.
  • The template includes two dummy rows (SP0001, SP0002) that you can upload as a test.
