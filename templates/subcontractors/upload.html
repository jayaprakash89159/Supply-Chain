Before you upload

  • Use the Labour & Composite sheet with the template's 42 columns, from Sl No to Remarks. A workbook with one sheet can use any sheet name.
  • Vendor Code and Vendor / Firm Name are required; existing vendor codes are rejected.
  • Trade and Project(s) Worked On accept any names; separate several with commas. New names are added automatically.
  • Labour Strength Available (Nos) is a whole number. Ratings: 0–5 for Quality, Timeliness, Safety and Overall. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Status: Active, On hold or Inactive. Checks: OK/Not OK or Yes/No.
  • The template includes one example row (SC0001) that you can upload as a test.
